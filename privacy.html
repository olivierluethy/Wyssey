Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 9, 2025

Wyssey respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

  • Messages you send in chats (stored locally in your browser unless you explicitly export/share them)
  • Custom characters you create (stored in localStorage)
  • Analytics events (via Google Analytics): page views, button clicks, character selections, scroll depth, time on site – no personal identifiers are sent
  • Technical data: IP address (anonymized), browser type, device type, approximate location (country level)

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Email address / name / phone number (unless voluntarily provided in chat – which we do not store server-side)
  • Payment information (no payments processed yet)
  • Full chat histories on our servers (everything stays in your browser)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use data to:

  • Operate and improve the Service
  • Understand usage patterns (anonymized analytics)
  • Debug technical issues

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google Analytics – anonymized usage statistics
  • OpenAI API or Anthropic Claude API – your messages are sent to these providers to generate replies. Please review their privacy policies.

5. Data Storage & Security

All conversation data is stored only in your browser (localStorage / IndexedDB). We do not store your chat histories on our servers. Custom characters and memory summaries also remain client-side.

6. Your Rights

You can:

  • Clear localStorage in your browser to delete all chats & characters
  • Opt out of analytics by using ad-blockers or privacy browsers
  • Contact us if you have questions
